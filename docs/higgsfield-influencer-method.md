# Higgsfield reference production method

Higgsfield is the default media platform for new OLYMPOX character images, references, scenes, voice, animation, video and lip-sync, subject to actual capability, cost and applicable authorization. Codex or Claude coordinates discovery, concept, personality, narrative, scripts, prompts and records; the standard method does not depend on integrated coordinator image generation. Follow the demonstrated reference process through verified modules, with [integrated images](integrated-images.md) available only as an explicit user-selected alternative. Work in an independent studio. Installation never connects an account or performs production, and a missing required capability stays pending rather than triggering a silent substitution.

## Explore a memorable concept before building

Begin a new OLYMPOX character with the [strategy onboarding conversation](strategy.md). Atena presents a small set of understandable goal/type options, such as viral entertainment, a recurring presenter, or a brand role, and asks only the unresolved choices that affect the concept. Reuse preferences already supplied and skip resolved discovery when the brief is complete. When direction remains open, present three concrete directions and a recommendation suited to that goal. Do not open a provider module with empty settings as a substitute for discovering the character's purpose.

For a new influencer, Gaia, Psiquê, and Íris connect the audience and recurring format to a recognizable visual idea. Propose useful alternatives rather than defaulting to a conventional attractive photoreal portrait. When memorable realism is selected, keep those alternatives realistic and distinguish them through strong personalities, presence, visual signatures, and recurring premises. A stylized or exaggerated original character is an option when the user's direction permits it; a mixture of fantasy and realism is not a required set of concepts. Explain each direction's personality, opening, recurring situations, and reason someone might watch or share. Viral potential remains a hypothesis to test with real content and results, never a guarantee.

The current [Builder description](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/new-ai-influencer) offers Average, Bold, and Extreme levels. Their [documented machine values](https://open.higgsfield.ai/models/higgsfield/ai-influencer/api-reference), also accepted in the inspected plugin, are `normal`, `freak`, and `total`. Choose deliberately from the brief; a tool default is not art direction. Boldness can come from silhouette, contrast, performance, a coherent fictional trait, or a surprising premise. Do not force unusual anatomy, random novelty, generic beauty, or one aesthetic on every character. Keep original adult identity and the chosen concept coherent across poses and scenes.

Present the options and recommendation before final identity selection. For an existing approved character, improve scripts, staging, permitted styling, and recurring formats within canon first. A desire for stronger viral potential does not authorize changing its face, body, voice, or approved anchors. Any proposed identity evolution remains a separate user decision.

An explicit request only to open the native Builder or another provider picker follows that tool's current exclusive-turn/display instructions. It does not by itself mean that OLYMPOX onboarding, character selection, approval, or the complete production method occurred. Distinguish the requested interface action from an actual framework character-creation task.

## Preserve an already approved character

Begin with the existing character ID, canon version, approval, snapshot when present, main reference, complementary references, and voice when already approved. Keep their stored bytes, hashes, approvals, and history intact. Preparing a Higgsfield representation of that identity is production work; it does not authorize a redesign, overwrite, new identity version, or repeated approval of unchanged canon.

The Higgsfield default does not retroactively recreate or migrate an approved character. Continue from its recorded method and approved context. If a different production route is selected, including Higgsfield/Builder preparation, preserve canon and inspect the new derivative references; do not treat that route change as identity evolution. Keep historical media, approvals, backups and runs intact; a context change uses a new attempt rather than rewritten evidence.

Map the exact approved main image to a confirmed provider identity media ID through the [supported transfer route](higgsfield-plugin.md#transfer-exact-studio-references). Record path, SHA-256, provider ID, and transfer evidence. Use the other approved views for fidelity inspection and any downstream tool that actually accepts them. Never combine several people into one Builder identity slot or use Style references as additional identity slots.

Keep any new sheet as a candidate derivative. Compare the face, age, proportions, marks, hair within permitted variations, and recognition across the required views. A mismatch calls for correction while canon stays preserved. Only an actual user decision to evolve identity creates a new canon version and approval through the existing process. Provider settings, seeds, successful jobs, and reference conditioning do not prove fidelity.

## When selected, prepare the Builder with current plugin tools

The following tool metadata was inspected on **October 8, 2026** without uploading media, connecting an account, generating, or spending credits. Read the actual schema again in the execution session. The names below identify plugin operations; they are not local OLYMPOX CLI commands or automatic adapters.

`ai_influencer_prepare` prepares a sheet design and returns exact `params`, total `credits` for the chosen `batch_size`, and free-generation availability when known. It submits no job. The inspected schema accepts at most one confirmed identity image in `medias`, up to three Style references in `item_medias`, and `batch_size` from one to four, default one. Style inputs guide items/presentation and must remain separate from identity.

For a preserved design, set `randomize: false`; the default is `true`, which fills unspecified traits. Select an explicit compatible `tier` instead of accidentally using the `freak` default applied when randomization is disabled. Preserve confirmed settings and seed when exposed; do not invent trait IDs or incompatible selections. A schematic preparation for an approved human identity in the ordinary tier is:

```json
{
  "tier": "normal",
  "randomize": false,
  "batch_size": 1,
  "brief": "Preserve the approved identity and permitted appearance.",
  "medias": [
    { "role": "image", "data": { "id": "CONFIRMED_IDENTITY_MEDIA_ID", "type": "media_input" } }
  ],
  "item_medias": []
}
```

Replace the placeholder only with the real confirmed ID; select settings that actually fit canon. A local Windows path is not a provider media ID. An unverified upload keeps preparation pending. Do not repeatedly prepare with randomization to refresh a price or recover a prior character.

`ai_influencer_generate` consumes **exactly the `params` returned by preparation**, unchanged, together with the applicable `max_credits`. Persist external intent first, then submit only within the user's authorization. The tool rechecks price and rejects prices above the cap. A quote is neither approval nor a total-pipeline budget. Paid generation spends existing credits; it does not purchase a subscription or credits.

For explicit free-only intent, the inspected generation schema requires `use_free_gens: true` with `max_credits: 0` and backend eligibility; it submits one sheet and never falls back to paid generation. Do not infer free intent or availability from missing price information. Preserve each returned `job_id`/`job_ids` and wait for every accepted job using the current `jobs_wait` limits (`timeout_seconds` at most 15). The generation tool already displays its jobs; do not open another display just to refresh them. Uncertain submission requires querying/reconciliation, never automatic retry.

For a saved sheet, `ai_influencer_get_settings` takes its real `job_id` and returns reusable settings, including identity/Style references when present. Pass those settings to `ai_influencer_prepare` with `randomize: false`; preserve explicit tier/appearance and choose the new batch size deliberately. Settings lookup neither regenerates nor saves a sheet, quotes a new job, replays the previous batch, or recreates missing local canon. Keep returned settings and output identifiers in local provenance before proceeding.

The [public API reference](https://open.higgsfield.ai/models/higgsfield/ai-influencer/api-reference) also separates identity guidance from clothing/item guidance. Its API input names, authentication, prices, and output limits are not interchangeable with plugin schemas or CLI options.

## Optional training and verified voice

Soul ID is a separate trained identity layer within compatible Higgsfield tools. The [official Soul ID guide](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/how-do-i-create-and-use-a-soul-id-character) describes a multi-photo training set and says the trained identity itself is not exported as a standalone file. A character sheet is not proof of training. The inspected session metadata did not expose a Soul ID training operation; do not fabricate a plugin call or report training complete. If required for the selected method, retain that stage pending until a real route, inputs, cost, and applicable authorization exist. Do not train automatically merely because an influencer will recur.

Voice is a separate asset and decision. The [official talking-avatar walkthrough](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/talking-ai-avatar-inside-claude) separates character, trained identity, voice, and the talking clip. For a new speaking character, prepare the reference sample before final canon approval; for an existing character, reuse approved voice when supported. In the inspected plugin, `list_voices` supplies the exact `voice_id` and `voice_type` pair; `generate_audio` submits speech and can perform a scoped cost preflight. Follow the current voice picker instructions when the pair is missing. Do not invent a voice ID, clone without applicable authorization, or interpret a voice label as proof that anyone listened. Record and inspect the spoken sample; a Builder image cannot approve vocal canon.

## Scenes, video, and honest delivery

When Higgsfield video is selected, Selene maps each shot to reviewed identity/start images and audio where required, supported media roles, model parameters, intended duration/format, and its own cost scope. Inspect `models_get` or equivalent current discovery when needed. `estimate_video_cost` is a video quote, not proof of ready inputs or completion of earlier stages; HTTPS references may be imported externally. `generate_video` submits video separately. Selecting Kling does not complete a Builder stage that the plan explicitly requires, train an identity, or create an approved voice. An explicitly chosen alternative with integrated references does not require a Builder stage unless the selected method requires one.

Preserve intent before each submission, record every accepted identifier, and reconcile outcomes under the [plugin run procedure](higgsfield-plugin.md#record-the-provider-outcome). Keep pending provider outcome, missing export, and missing inspection distinguishable. Follow provider instructions for animation/preset choice; a completed sheet alone does not authorize another paid job or automatic motion-preset selection.

Têmis inspects images visually, listens to audio, and reviews complete video motion and audio against the exact canon, script, and intended use. Save actual local outputs, register them, preserve generation context through `execution-seal`, and bind the review to those files/hashes. Edited, captioned, cropped, or recompressed exports require a new review. If export or full audiovisual access is missing, retain `pending`/`awaiting-tool` and deliver the prepared package plus the exact outstanding stage. Tests, widget previews, and provider success do not establish fidelity, listening, training, or publication.
